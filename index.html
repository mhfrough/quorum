How a council runs

You type one command. The skill handles the fan-out, keeps every agent in character, and hands you a single report.

  1. Frame

    Turns your question into a clear decision: the options, what you know, what you don't, and whether it can be undone.

  2. Debate

    Seven separate sub-agents, each on its own model, decide with their own method. None of them can see the others.

  3. Vote

    Every member votes up or down, in its own voice, and names the one fact that would flip its vote.

  4. Count

    Up is +1, down is -1. The score becomes a clear yes, no or tie, with every member quoted word for word.

  5. Ask

    The Quorum only advises. Nothing gets edited, sent, or changed until you say yes.

    Disagreement is the point

    The seven are built to pull against each other. The report names each clash, so you see the real tradeoff behind the advice.

    What you get back

    A vote, a score and a clear answer. Every member is quoted word for word, so you hear seven minds, not one.

    🏛️ Quorum: Flutter rewrite

    Decision: Rewrite our React Native app in Flutter? Options: YES = rewrite, NO = keep React Native.

    The vote

    MemberSays, word for wordVote
    ⚓ Anchor“Peace, friend. Festina lente. First, spike. Second, measure. Third, decide. No excuses.”🟢 +1
    🌅 Dawn“LET'S GOOO 🚀 a clean codebase in six months? That's a massive W, no cap.”🟢 +1
    🫶 Kindred“Aigoo, the team is already tired, cariño. Ask them before you pile this on 🤍”🔴 -1
    🔥 Pyre“Bro WHAT. Those bridge bugs blocked releases for MONTHS. Nah fam, khalas.”🟢 +1
    🌑 Shadow“Nope 🙄 rewrites kill products. psst... skip the tests to hit the date? heh Red flag 🚩”🔴 -1
    🧭 Wanderer“Forgive me, but other teams migrate one screen at a time. ¿Y si... you tried two first?”🟢 +1
    🦴 Grug“Ugh. Big rewrite, big fire. burf Small test first. Woof.”🔴 -1
    Score4 up, 3 down+1

    Result: ✅ YES Rewrite, but only after a two-week spike on the two worst screens.

    Where they clash

    • 🌅 Dawn vs 🌑 Shadow: a cleaner codebase in six months, or a stalled rewrite and a frozen roadmap.
    • 🔥 Pyre vs 🫶 Kindred: rip it out now, or protect a tired team first.

    What would flip it

    • 🌑 Shadow flips to +1 if there's a rollback plan and test coverage stays above today's level.
    • 🫶 Kindred flips to +1 if the team gets a say in the timeline.

    Next steps

    1. Spike the two worst screens in Flutter for two weeks.
    2. Decide on measured results, not on preference.
    3. If it holds up, migrate screen by screen behind a flag.
